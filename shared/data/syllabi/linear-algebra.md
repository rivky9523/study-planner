# Linear Algebra 1 (linear-algebra)
lecturer: Dr. Cohen | credits: 5 | semester: Winter 2026-27

## Grading
- homework: 10% (must submit 8 of 10 exercises)
- midterm: 20% (optional, "magen" - counts only if it raises the grade)
- final exam: 70-90%

## Exam topics
- systems of linear equations, Gaussian elimination
- vector spaces, subspaces, span, linear independence
- basis and dimension
- linear transformations, kernel and image
- matrices: rank, inverse, determinants
- NOT on the exam: eigenvalues (moved to Linear Algebra 2)

## Homework policy
- exercises are released on Sunday and due 10 days later
- late submission: minus 10 points per day, up to 3 days
